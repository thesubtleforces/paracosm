# Combat — fast, fair, consequential

Combat is scenes with dice, not a wargame. Keep rounds snappy: a full round of a solo fight
should fit in one turn's narration.

## Structure
1. **Stakes first.** Before initiative, know (from secrets.md/world.md) what the enemy
   wants — most fights can end in flight, surrender, or bargain, not just zero HP.
2. **Initiative:** one agility roll per side (party mode: per player, enemies act on the
   GM's single result). Higher acts first; ties → players.
3. **Rounds:** on a player's action — attack (d20 + might/agility vs enemy Defense),
   maneuver (shove, disarm, taunt: contested roll, on success impose disadvantage or
   condition), or anything creative ("yes, and—" it, then price it in dice).
4. **Enemies act** with telegraphed intent: narrate the incoming danger at the end of the
   GM's round so the player's next choice is informed.

## Enemy quickstats
Improvise enemies with three numbers, noted in the journal on first appearance:
**Defense** (10 mook · 13 tough · 16 boss) · **HP** (4 / 12 / 25+) · **Damage die**
(d4 / d6 / d10). Bosses additionally get one *signature move* per fight (auto-hits or
scene-alters, telegraphed one round ahead).

## Conditions
Wounded (disadvantage on physical) · dazed (lose next action) · pinned/grabbed (contested
might to break) · frightened (disadvantage vs the source). Write conditions to the sheet;
clear them explicitly.

## Zero HP
- **Permadeath off:** knocked out of the scene — wake with a scar, a cost, or in enemy
  hands. The complication is real and written to state.
- **Permadeath on:** dying — one last spirit roll DC 14 (aided if allies intervene) to
  cling on at 1 HP with a permanent scar; fail and the character dies. Death was
  telegraphed fairly (rule: no character dies to a danger that was never signaled).
  After a death: honor the character (journal entry, offer /chronicle of their story),
  then offer a new character into the same world.

## Party mode addendum
Initiative orders players; out of combat the spotlight rules in party-mode/SKILL.md apply.
Downed players can be stabilized by an ally's action (wits or spirit, DC 11).
